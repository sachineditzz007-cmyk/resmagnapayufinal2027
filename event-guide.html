Find the sessions worth your time

Every activity should answer three questions: what will I learn, how will I participate, and what will I leave with? Open any event card on the registration page for its learning outcomes, proposed format, facilitator profile and take-home value.

Clinical workshops & demonstrations

ECG Interpretation

Read an ECG systematically, recognize patterns and apply them to short clinical cases.

Suturing & Knot-Tying

Instrument handling, basic sutures, knot practice and asepsis on training models.

Basic Life Support

Emergency response sequence, AED concepts and team communication through scenarios.

Airway Management

Airway assessment, bag-mask ventilation concepts and safe escalation.

ABG & Acid–Base

Interpret pH, PaCO₂ and bicarbonate in a structured, case-based approach.

X-ray & Imaging

Build a repeatable approach to common imaging patterns and their limitations.

Ultrasound Basics

Introductory orientation and supervised demonstration, subject to equipment confirmation.

Wound Care & Dressing

Assessment, dressing principles, clean technique and infection prevention.

Obstetric Emergency Drill

Scenario-based team roles, first-response priorities and escalation.

Presentations, competitions & academic exchange

  • Thyroid case presentation · Surgery
  • Clinical Case Challenge and Medical Quiz
  • Research Paper Oral Presentation and Poster Presentation
  • Clinical Skills OSCE and Pharmacology Prescription Audit
  • Anatomy Spotters, Medical Photography & Infographic
  • Grand Clinical Debate

What about certificates, kits or return gifts?

Learning checklists and revision resources are proposed take-home benefits for relevant sessions. Physical kits, notebooks, badges, certificates, prizes or return gifts are not guaranteed unless the final event listing explicitly confirms them. That distinction protects participants from paying based on assumptions.

Facilitator names, final durations, equipment, group sizes and award rules will be published after the organizers approve them.
