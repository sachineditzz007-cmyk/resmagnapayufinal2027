Speaker announcements · RESMAGNA 2027

Meet the people behind the learning

A good workshop depends on the person guiding it, the practice available and the feedback participants receive. The programme is designed around relevant clinical faculty, trained skills instructors and academic moderators.

Planned facilitator profiles

Clinical skills & emergency workshops

Relevant clinical faculty and appropriately trained instructors for BLS, airway, suturing, wound care and simulation sessions.

Academic sessions

Faculty moderators and reviewers for case presentations, research talks, posters and prescription review.

Competitions

Quizmasters, station assessors, judges and moderators using published rules and scoring criteria.

Debate & research

Moderators and reviewers who can help participants engage with evidence, uncertainty and scientific communication.

Why aren’t individual names listed yet?

Names should appear here only after each facilitator has confirmed participation and the organizing team has approved the announcement. Listing unconfirmed names would be misleading. The event cards currently describe the intended facilitator profile, not a promise that a particular person has accepted.

What to expect before booking: Confirmed speaker names, session format, duration, prerequisites and any included materials should be added to the relevant event card as soon as the organizing team finalizes them.
