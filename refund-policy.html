RESMAGNA 2027 · Proposed terms for organizer review

Cancellation, changes & refunds

Important: draft policy, not yet approved. The refund schedule below is a professional proposal for the organizing team to approve or replace. It is not a binding promise until the organizers formally approve and publish the final policy. Before accepting live payments, replace this notice with the approved policy and ensure it matches the checkout terms.

We understand that plans can change. The goal of this proposed policy is to set expectations clearly, protect participants from avoidable uncertainty and give the organizing team a consistent way to handle requests.

1. Participant cancellation (proposed)

14 or more calendar days before the event

Proposed refund: 90% of the eligible registration fee, less any non-refundable payment-gateway charges actually applied.

7–13 calendar days before the event

Proposed refund: 50% of the eligible registration fee.

0–6 calendar days before the event or no-show

Proposed policy: registration fees are non-refundable because venue, materials and catering commitments may already have been made.

Duplicate or confirmed failed payment

Proposed handling: duplicate charges or a payment confirmed as failed should be reconciled and returned in full where the provider confirms funds were captured.

Cancellation requests should be sent from the registered email address to sachinst557@gmail.com with the participant name, registration reference, transaction ID, selected events and reason for cancellation. Do not include card numbers, passwords or gateway secrets.

2. Event cancellation or material change by organizers

Proposed approach: if the organizers cancel the entire event, refund the eligible event registration fees in full. If an individual paid session is cancelled and no equivalent alternative is accepted, refund the fee for that session. If dates or venue change materially, participants should receive notice and a clear choice to accept the change or request a refund for affected paid items.

3. Food and accommodation add-ons

Proposed approach: refund eligibility for food and accommodation should depend on the cancellation date and whether non-recoverable supplier costs have been committed. The final policy should specify the exact cut-off and any deductions before payment is enabled.

4. Refund processing

After approval, the proposed service target is to initiate eligible refunds within 7–10 business days. The time taken for funds to appear depends on PayU, the bank and the original payment method. The team should provide a reference or update if processing takes longer.

5. How to request help

Email sachinst557@gmail.com with the transaction reference and a short description. The organizer should acknowledge the request, verify the payment record and respond in writing. A screenshot alone is not proof of payment or refund.

6. Before paying

Check the approved cancellation terms, final event fees, add-on conditions, speaker/session details and venue information. The organizers should publish the final approved policy on this page and in the registration flow before accepting real payments.

Organizer checklist before launch: Approve or edit the proposed refund percentages and cut-off dates; define treatment of gateway fees and add-ons; confirm response/refund processing targets; and ensure the same policy appears at checkout.
