3–5 January 2027 · Proposed programme · Conducting batch: SKARZXXIINOZZ’ 22

Learn together. Practise thoughtfully. Leave more confident.

RESMAGNA 2027 is being prepared as a three-day intercollegiate medical learning event, bringing together clinical workshops, academic presentations and friendly competitions. The aim is to make the time useful: build a practical skill, sharpen clinical reasoning, learn from peers and take home ideas you can revisit during your training.

🩺 Clinical skills

Proposed sessions include ECG interpretation, suturing, basic life support, airway basics, ABG interpretation, imaging, wound care and obstetric emergency simulation.

🧠 Academic exchange

Case presentations, research talks, posters, prescription review and an evidence-based debate create space to explain your thinking and learn from feedback.

🏆 Friendly challenges

Clinical case challenges, quizzes, OSCE-style stations, anatomy spotters and visual communication invite participants to test themselves.

🤝 Meet your peers

Share approaches, compare perspectives across colleges and build connections around medicine and medical learning.
