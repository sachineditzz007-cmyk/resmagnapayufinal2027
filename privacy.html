Privacy notice

For RESMAGNA 2027 · 3–5 January 2027 · Government Thoothukudi Medical College

This notice explains how information submitted through the RESMAGNA 2027 registration page is intended to be used. Please contact the organizers before registering if you have questions about your information.

Organizer review: This is the registration site's working privacy notice. The organizers should confirm the final retention period, responsible contact and operational data-sharing arrangements before accepting live registrations.

Information collected

The registration form may collect your name, email address, mobile number, college or institution, course/year, gender, food preference, selected events, meal or accommodation add-ons, transaction references and payment status.

Why it is used

  • To create and manage your event registration and selected activities.
  • To calculate fees, initiate checkout and reconcile payment results.
  • To send registration updates, payment status messages and, after verified payment, attendance or entry information.
  • To help authorized organizers prepare attendee lists, event-wise lists, meal and accommodation counts, and attendance records.
  • To investigate payment disputes, prevent misuse and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Payments

Online checkout is handed off to PayU. Payment credentials entered on PayU's hosted checkout are handled under PayU's own terms and privacy notice. RESMAGNA's registration system stores the registration amount, transaction identifiers, payment status and relevant response metadata for reconciliation; it is not intended to collect or store your full card number or banking password.

Service providers and organizer access

Registration information may be processed by the database, email and organizer-management services configured for this site, including Supabase and any enabled spreadsheet or Airtable synchronization. Access should be limited to authorized event organizers and service providers who need the information to operate registration, payments, communications or attendance.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as needed to administer the event, resolve payment questions and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations, then securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate. The organizers should define and apply the final retention schedule. No online service can promise absolute security, but access credentials and service permissions should be kept restricted and never shared publicly.

Your choices and requests

You may contact the organizers to ask about the information associated with your registration or request correction of inaccurate details. Some payment or accounting records may need to be retained where required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

Contact

For registration and privacy questions, email sachinst557@gmail.com and include your registration reference if you have one. Do not email passwords, PayU salt values or full payment credentials.
