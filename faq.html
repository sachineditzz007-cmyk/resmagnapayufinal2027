Participant help · Updated for the current proposed programme

Before you book, here’s what to know

What are the event dates and venue?

The proposed dates are 3–5 January 2027 at Government Thoothukudi Medical College. Confirm the final venue instructions and timetable with the organizing team before travel.

Are all listed fees final?

No. The site currently labels event prices and capacities as illustrative/sample values. Organizers must approve the final fees, add-ons and availability before live registration is treated as open.

How do I choose events?

Tap a session card to see its format, learning outcomes, proposed facilitator profile and take-home value. You cannot add two sessions that overlap in the same day and time slot.

Who will teach the workshops?

Facilitator profiles are shown on the event cards. Individual names will be added only after confirmed acceptance and organizer approval.

Are return gifts, certificates or prizes included?

Only when the final event listing explicitly says so. Learning checklists or revision resources are proposed for some sessions; physical gifts, kits, certificates and prizes should not be assumed unless confirmed.

How does payment work?

Checkout is handed off to PayU. Do not treat a browser redirect or screenshot alone as proof of payment. Wait for the registration status confirmation and retain your transaction reference.

What if money is debited but my registration is pending?

Do not pay repeatedly. Save the transaction reference, date/time and amount, then email sachinst557@gmail.com. The team should reconcile the transaction with the payment provider before advising next steps.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Review the cancellation and refund policy page before payment. The proposed schedule on that page is still subject to formal organizer approval and must not be treated as binding until the approved terms are published.

Is accommodation guaranteed?

No. Accommodation options and prices are shown as sample values and depend on final availability, room arrangements and organizer confirmation.

How is my personal information used?

Read the privacy notice. The organizers should confirm the final data-retention period and responsible contact before live registrations.

Who can I contact?

Email sachinst557@gmail.com. Include your registration or transaction reference when available, but never send passwords, card details or payment gateway secrets.
